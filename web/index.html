How the pipeline works

Six stages turn a raw file into grounded answers. The same extracted text also powers the resume assessment.

  1. 1

    Extract

    Pull text from PDF, DOCX, TXT and MD while keeping page numbers.

  2. 2

    Chunk

    Split on paragraphs with overlap so ideas are not cut mid-sentence.

  3. 3

    Embed

    Convert each chunk to a vector. Works offline; swap in a neural model for higher recall.

  4. 4

    Retrieve

    Rank chunks by cosine similarity to the question and keep the top matches.

  5. 5

    Answer

    Generate an answer from retrieved context only, with citations. Refuses when the answer is absent.

  6. 6

    Coach

    Detect resume skills, test them with MCQs, score, and map a learning route.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my documents used to train models?

    No. Text is processed in memory for retrieval and scoring. Nothing is sent to a third party unless you configure an LLM key yourself.

    Do answers work without an AI key?

    Yes. Without a key, answers are the most relevant sentences from your document with page citations. With an Anthropic key set, answers are written by the model from the same retrieved context.

    Why are questions multiple choice?

    Multiple-choice items can be scored consistently and explained immediately, which makes the guidance specific.
